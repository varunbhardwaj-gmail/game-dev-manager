# DelhiWalker - reference captures

Street-level reference photos for the opening area of DelhiWalker, in two route folders:

| Folder | Covers |
|---|---|
| `Route1_Entry_to_Flyover\` | Entering Gurugram on NH-48, straight up and over the Shankar Chowk flyover, stopping just after it |
| `Route2_Entry_to_Building5\` | Same entry, left before the flyover, curving left into Cyber City to DLF Building No. 5, plus Building 5 from all available angles |

`INVENTORY.md` lists every photo by route and section, with position, direction, date, author and links.

## Files here
- `capture.bat`: downloads or refreshes all photos and rebuilds INVENTORY.md (`capture.bat r1_main` redoes one section)
- `capture_plan.json`: routes, waypoints and filters
- `survey.bat`: maps where Mapillary photos exist (no downloads) and opens coverage_map.html
- `route_preview.html`: photo coverage with the current routes drawn on top
- `capture_log.txt`, `survey_log.txt`: output of the last runs

## Notes
- Route 1 uses a 2016 drive, the only one that stays on top of the flyover. Expect newer buildings today.
- Building 5 coverage is limited to what Mapillary contributors shot; sides with no photos need your own.
